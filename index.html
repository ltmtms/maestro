How to conduct
  • Start like a real conductor: a preparatory upbeat ↑, then the downbeat ↓. The time between them sets the tempo.
  • Follow the golden arrows. Each beat is a stroke in the arrow’s direction; the light reaches the end on the beat.
  • Size is volume: small gestures for piano, big ones for forte. Match the marking on the right.
  • The orchestra follows you. Speed up and it speeds up; stop, and it will wait for your next beat.
  • Baton: a real baton, a chopstick or a pencil. Light-coloured against a dark background is easiest to see. No baton? Your hand works too.
